# Market Research: ClassLite Competitive Pricing for IELTS Learning Centers in Vietnam

**Date:** 2026-02-27
**Author:** Ducdo
**Research Type:** Market Research — Competitive Pricing Analysis

---

## Executive Summary

This research analyzed the competitive pricing landscape for ClassLite's flat-rate SaaS pricing (Starter $20, Growth $50, Enterprise $100/month) targeting small-to-medium IELTS learning centers in Vietnam. Key findings:

- **ClassLite's pricing is competitive internationally** — cheaper than most global tutoring management tools at meaningful scale
- **ClassLite's pricing is expensive by Vietnamese local standards** — local tools like Easy Edu (~$12/mo for 5 users) and MISA EMIS (~$7/mo/module) anchor expectations significantly lower
- **Flat pricing is a genuine differentiator** — most competitors use confusing per-student, per-lesson, or revenue-sharing models
- **No IELTS-specific competitor exists** — this is ClassLite's strongest potential differentiator
- **The real competition is "free"** — Google Sheets and manual processes remain the dominant "tool" for many small centers
- **Recommendation**: Consider a Vietnam-specific pricing strategy with a lower entry point (~$9-12/mo) to compete with local tools, while preserving international pricing for other markets

## Customer Insights

### Customer Behavior Patterns

The Vietnamese IELTS market is experiencing what researchers call **"IELTSification"** — a fundamental shift where IELTS preparation has become deeply embedded in the education system:

- **62% of IELTS test takers** are now aged 16-22, with the 16-18 age group growing **20x since 2018** — this means centers are serving younger students in larger volumes
- **30+ universities** now use IELTS scores for admissions, and the Ministry of Education grants **exam exemption (automatic score of 10/10)** for students with IELTS 4.0+
- **~450 English language centers** operate in just Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City alone, with demand spreading to rural areas
- Centers report being **"fully booked" with weeks-long waiting lists**

_Sources: [VietnamNet — Vietnam IELTS scores 2023-2024](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/vietnam-s-average-ielts-score-falls-in-2023-2024-only-1-score-above-8-5-2331801.html), [RMIT Vietnam — IELTSification](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/news/all-news/2024/mar/the-ielts-ification-of-english-language-teaching-in-vietnam), [VnExpress — English centers booming](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/trend/english-centers-laugh-all-the-way-to-the-bank-on-booming-ielts-demand-4453991.html)_

### Pain Points and Challenges

For small-to-medium IELTS centers, the operational challenges center around:

- **Student volume management** — booming demand means more students to track, schedule, and bill
- **Financial tracking** — IELTS courses range from **2.5M VND/month (~$100)** to **5M VND for a 72-hour course (~$215)**; parents invest **50M+ VND ($2,150+)** per student over their IELTS journey
- **Technology adoption barriers** — the biggest obstacle for Vietnamese businesses is not cost but **adaptation and training staff on new processes**
- **Budget consciousness** — 97% of Vietnamese businesses are SMEs, and they are described as having "budget constraints" when adopting cloud services

_Sources: [VnExpress — English centers booming](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/trend/english-centers-laugh-all-the-way-to-the-bank-on-booming-ielts-demand-4453991.html), [Akabot — SaaS Landscape Vietnam](https://akabot.com/additional-resources/blog/the-landscape-of-saas-in-viet-nam-and-future-prediction), [Vietnam Briefing — English Education Market](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnams-english-education-market-2023.html/)_

### Decision-Making Processes

Vietnamese education center owners making software decisions are influenced by:

- **Price-first evaluation** — local Vietnamese software starts at strikingly low price points (e.g., Easy Edu at **~2,000 VND/day/user ≈ $2.40/user/month**), setting very low price anchors
- **Vietnamese-language preference** — local solutions like Easy Edu (1,500+ centers), CenterOnline (350+ centers), MISA EMIS, and others all offer full Vietnamese UIs
- **"Contact us" pricing culture** — most Vietnamese education software does NOT publish prices publicly, relying on sales consultations, which suggests price negotiation is expected
- **Government support awareness** — the 2024 SME Tech-Loan Fund offers favorable interest rates (80% of lowest commercial rates) for technology adoption

_Sources: [Easy Edu](https://easyedu.vn/), [Flyer.vn — Top 11 English Center Software](https://flyer.vn/phan-mem-quan-ly-trung-tam-tieng-anh/), [Library of Congress — Vietnam SME Financial Support](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2024-07-15/vietnam-financial-support-program-for-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises-amended/)_

### Strengths and Weaknesses

**ClassLite's Competitive Strengths:**
- **Flat pricing is a major advantage** — no per-student, per-lesson, or revenue % surprises. Centers know exactly what they'll pay
- **Starter at $20 is competitive internationally** — cheaper than Teach 'n Go, TutorCruncher, and Teachworks at any meaningful scale
- **Growth at $50 is reasonable** — still cheaper than most international tools when factoring in usage-based fees
- **No hidden costs** — compared to Teachworks' per-lesson fees or TutorCruncher's revenue sharing

**ClassLite's Competitive Weaknesses:**
- **Vietnamese local software is 2-5x cheaper** — Easy Edu at ~$12/mo for 5 users vs ClassLite Starter at $20
- **Vietnamese UI/UX expectation** — local tools speak Vietnamese natively; if ClassLite doesn't, this is a barrier
- **"Free" is the real competitor** — many small centers use Google Sheets and feel it's "good enough"
- **Enterprise at $100 faces stiff competition** — international tools at this price offer significantly more features (API access, multi-branch, advanced reporting)

### Market Differentiation Opportunities

1. **IELTS-specific features** — NO competitor specifically targets IELTS centers. This is a blue ocean niche
2. **Vietnamese market localization** — full Vietnamese UI + VND pricing would massively reduce friction
3. **Flat pricing simplicity** — a genuine differentiator vs the confusing per-lesson/per-student models
4. **Parent communication tools** — Vietnamese parents spend 24% of their budget on education and are deeply invested; tools that help them track progress are high-value

### Competitive Threats

1. **Easy Edu's dominance** — 1,500+ centers at dramatically lower prices with full Vietnamese support [High Confidence]
2. **Free tool inertia** — convincing centers to move from Google Sheets requires demonstrating clear ROI [High Confidence]
3. **CenterOnline's language-center focus** — purpose-built for exactly this market, with 350+ centers already [Medium Confidence]
4. **Price sensitivity** — Vietnam average salary is $666/month; even $20/month feels more significant than in Western markets [High Confidence]

### Competitive Opportunities

1. **No IELTS-specific leader exists** — the center management space is generic; an IELTS-optimized tool could own this niche
2. **Market is growing fast** — "IELTSification" means more centers opening, all needing management tools
3. **VND pricing could unlock the market** — pricing in local currency (e.g., 250,000 VND instead of "$10") reduces psychological friction
4. **Government digitization push** — the national digital transformation program + SME Tech-Loan Fund create favorable tailwinds

## Strategic Synthesis & Recommendations

### The Core Pricing Tension

ClassLite faces a classic **market entry pricing dilemma** in Vietnam:

- **International positioning**: At $20/$50/$100, ClassLite is well-priced against global competitors — cheaper than Teach 'n Go, Teachworks at scale, and TutorCruncher
- **Local positioning**: At $20/$50/$100, ClassLite is 2-5x more expensive than Vietnamese local tools like Easy Edu (~$12/mo) and MISA EMIS (~$7/mo)

The question is: **should ClassLite price to compete with local tools or with international tools?**

### Recommendation: Vietnam-Specific Pricing Tier

**Recommended approach**: Introduce a Vietnam-localized pricing structure while keeping international pricing for other markets.

#### Option A: Aggressive Vietnam Pricing (Recommended)

| Tier | Vietnam Price (USD) | Vietnam Price (VND) | International Price |
|------|-------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Starter | **$9** | **~225,000 VND** | $20 |
| Growth | **$25** | **~625,000 VND** | $50 |
| Enterprise | **$49** | **~1,225,000 VND** | $100 |

**Rationale:**
- Starter at $9 is competitive with Easy Edu ($12 for 5 users) and below the psychological $10 barrier
- Growth at $25 is half the current price but still provides clear upgrade value
- Enterprise at $49 stays under $50 — a key psychological barrier in emerging markets
- VND pricing removes the "foreign software" perception

#### Option B: Moderate Vietnam Adjustment

| Tier | Vietnam Price (USD) | International Price |
|------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Starter | **$15** | $20 |
| Growth | **$35** | $50 |
| Enterprise | **$70** | $100 |

**Rationale:**
- Smaller adjustments, preserving more margin
- Risk: still more expensive than local tools, may not be enough to overcome the price gap

#### Option C: Keep Current Pricing, Differentiate on Value

| Tier | Price | Strategy |
|------|-------|----------|
| Starter | $20 | Lean into IELTS-specific features as the premium differentiator |
| Growth | $50 | Position as "the only tool built specifically for IELTS centers" |
| Enterprise | $100 | Target medium centers with 200+ students where $100 is <0.5% of revenue |

**Rationale:**
- Avoids a race to the bottom against local tools
- Risk: may struggle to acquire initial customers against $7-12/mo alternatives

### Revenue Impact Analysis

For a target of 100 Vietnamese customers:

| Scenario | Avg Revenue/Customer | Monthly Revenue | Annual Revenue |
|----------|---------------------|----------------|----------------|
| Current pricing (60% Starter, 30% Growth, 10% Enterprise) | $31 | $3,100 | $37,200 |
| Option A (60% Starter, 30% Growth, 10% Enterprise) | $15.30 | $1,530 | $18,360 |
| Option B (60% Starter, 30% Growth, 10% Enterprise) | $22 | $2,200 | $26,400 |

**Key consideration**: Option A generates ~50% less revenue per customer, BUT if the lower price drives 2-3x more adoption, total revenue could exceed current pricing. In a market with 450+ centers in HCMC and Hanoi alone, capturing even 10% would mean 45+ paying customers.

### Supporting Strategic Recommendations

1. **Price in VND, not USD** — Display prices as "225,000 VND/tháng" rather than "$9/month." This removes the "foreign premium" psychological barrier and feels local. [High Confidence]

2. **Offer annual discounts** — Vietnamese business culture values long-term commitment discounts. Offer 2 months free on annual plans (matching Teach 'n Go and local competitors). [High Confidence]

3. **Free trial with Vietnamese onboarding** — The #1 adoption barrier is training, not cost. Offer a generous free trial (14-30 days) with Vietnamese-language onboarding materials. [High Confidence]

4. **IELTS-specific positioning** — No competitor owns "IELTS center management." Position ClassLite as "Phần mềm quản lý trung tâm IELTS" (IELTS center management software) — not generic education software. [Medium-High Confidence]

5. **Consider a free tier** — Even a very limited free tier (e.g., up to 20 students) could help overcome the "Google Sheets is free" barrier and create a conversion funnel. ClassIn and eSkooly both offer free tiers. [Medium Confidence]

6. **Leverage the flat-pricing advantage in marketing** — Explicitly compare against per-student and per-lesson models. Show a calculator: "At 200 students, Teach 'n Go costs $97/mo. ClassLite: $25/mo flat. No surprises." [High Confidence]

### Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Local competitors undercut on price | High | Medium | Differentiate on IELTS-specific features, not price alone |
| Low conversion from free/Sheets users | High | High | Offer free tier + demonstrate clear ROI over spreadsheets |
| Exchange rate volatility (VND pricing) | Medium | Low | Peg VND prices and review quarterly |
| Cannibalization of international pricing | Low | Medium | Geo-restrict Vietnam pricing via billing address |

### Student Cap Analysis & Recommendation

ClassLite's current student caps per tier are:

| Tier | Current Student Cap | Current Price |
|------|-------------------|---------------|
| Starter | 30 students | $20/mo |
| Growth | 100 students | $50/mo |
| Enterprise | unlimited | $100/mo |

#### The Problem: 30-Student Starter Cap Is Too Low for Vietnam

Research shows the typical **small** IELTS center in Vietnam has **~50+ active students**, with centers frequently reported as "fully booked with weeks-long waiting lists." This means:

- **Almost no real operating IELTS center fits in Starter** — a center with 30 students is either brand-new (first 1-3 months) or a very small private tutor
- **Starter becomes a trial tier, not a working tier** — centers will hit the 30-student cap quickly in this booming market and be forced to upgrade
- **Effective entry price becomes $50 (Growth)** — which is 4x more expensive than Easy Edu (~$12/mo), making conversion very difficult

#### Competitor Student Cap Comparison

| Competitor | Lowest Tier Student Limit | Price |
|------------|--------------------------|-------|
| **Teach 'n Go** | 100 students | €69/mo (~$75) |
| **Teachworks** | Unlimited (per-lesson fees) | $16.49/mo base |
| **TutorBird** | Unlimited (per-user pricing) | $14.95/mo |
| **Easy Edu** | Unlimited (per-user/day pricing) | ~$12/mo for 5 users |

No major competitor caps their lowest tier at 30 students. Teach 'n Go's lowest tier starts at 100.

#### Revenue-per-Student Economics at Each Tier

| Scenario | Students | Center Revenue | ClassLite Cost | % of Revenue | Cost per Student |
|----------|----------|---------------|----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Starter (30 cap) | 30 | $3,000/mo | $20/mo | 0.67% | $0.67 |
| Growth (100 cap) | 50 | $5,000/mo | $50/mo | 1.0% | $1.00 |
| Growth (100 cap) | 100 | $10,000/mo | $50/mo | 0.5% | $0.50 |
| Enterprise | 150 | $15,000/mo | $100/mo | 0.67% | $0.67 |

Cost-to-value ratios are all reasonable. The problem is not affordability — it's the **forced upgrade cliff at 30 students** pushing centers past the entry price point too early.

#### Recommended Student Caps

| Tier | Current Cap | Recommended Cap | Rationale |
|------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Starter** | 30 | **50** | Captures the true "small center" in Vietnam (50 students); gives 6-12 months of runway before upgrade needed |
| **Growth** | 100 | **200** | Matches Teach 'n Go's second tier (€89/200 students) at a lower price; covers the majority of small-to-medium centers |
| **Enterprise** | unlimited | **unlimited** | Serves medium centers (200-300+ students) with room to grow |

**Why 50 for Starter**: A center with 50 students at $100/month tuition = $5,000/month revenue. Even at the current $20/month price, that's only 0.4% of revenue — very affordable. And critically, 50 students is the natural size of a real small IELTS center, making Starter a **genuine working tier** rather than a trial.

**Why 200 for Growth**: Doubling the Growth cap from 100 to 200 creates a much wider "comfort zone" for growing centers. A center crossing 100 students is making $10,000+/month — the jump to Enterprise is justifiable. But giving them room to 200 students means they stay on Growth longer, increasing satisfaction and reducing churn from "forced upgrade" frustration.

### Conclusion

ClassLite's current pricing ($20/$50/$100) is **well-positioned internationally but overpriced for the Vietnamese market** when compared to local alternatives. The current 30-student Starter cap compounds this problem by making the effective entry price $50/month (Growth) for most real IELTS centers.

The Vietnamese IELTS center market is large (450+ centers in major cities alone), growing fast (driven by government policy and cultural demand), and underserved by IELTS-specific tools.

**The strongest path forward is Option A** — aggressive Vietnam-specific pricing ($9/$25/$49) combined with raised student caps (50/200/unlimited) and IELTS-specific positioning with VND currency display. This positions ClassLite:
- Below or at parity with local Vietnamese tools
- Far below international competitors at scale
- As the only IELTS-specific center management solution
- With a Starter tier that actually serves real small centers

The trade-off is lower per-customer revenue, but the addressable market is large enough that volume can compensate. A center paying $9/month that stays for 2 years is worth $216 — far more than a center that never converts because $20/month felt too expensive compared to Easy Edu at $12, or because the 30-student cap forced them into a $50/month tier before they were ready.
